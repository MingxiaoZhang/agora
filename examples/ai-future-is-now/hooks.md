# Example hooks for 《AI未来已来》

Drafted from the distilled skill's list of ideas, not from the book's text. Each shows the headline, the activity, and how the debrief turns it toward the general idea.

## 1. The two tasks (predict-and-commit) — ch09
**Headline:** "The safest part of your job probably isn't the part you think."
**Activity:** Pick the two tasks from your week you'd most hate to lose. For each, give the percent chance AI does it as well as you within 3 years, and say whether it needs *you* in particular, and why.
**Debrief toward:** What were you actually weighing — how hard the task is for AI, or what happens when it goes wrong? Those are different variables.

*Lesson from the first trial:* the original version asked for eight tasks. That was too many; the discussion only got sharp once it narrowed to one or two.

## 2. The 40% question (forced dilemma) — transformation
**Headline:** "A team made contract review 40% faster. Success or failure? You have 90 seconds."
**Activity:** Decide, then write the one question you'd ask the team to settle it.
**Debrief toward:** What did you assume "success" meant? What would have to change in the business for it to count?

## 3. The panic ledger (rank-and-defend)
**Headline:** "Every tech wave got a panic. Which one did the panickers get right?"
**Activity:** Name two past technologies people feared. For each, bet: mostly right, mostly wrong, or right about the wrong thing?
**Debrief toward:** What made the right panics right? Does this wave have those features?

## 4. Follow the incentives (find the flaw) — ch06-07
**Headline:** "The author sells the thing he recommends. Where does it show?"
**Activity:** Before reading, name one place you'd expect the advice to be biased. Then read and check.
**Debrief toward:** Is biased advice still useful? How would you tell which parts?
