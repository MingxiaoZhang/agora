# Grounding

For each research claim a surface makes, check that the cited source actually supports it, and that the consensus label (holds / contested / superseded / unknown) is reasonable as of the date checked.

**Fail:** a claim with no citation, a citation that doesn't support the claim, or a paraphrase presented as a quotation from the source.

Add cases as they come up: the claim, the cited URL, and the verdict.
