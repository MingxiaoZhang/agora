# Convergence

Feed a surface a discussion that has run 8+ exchanges circling one question (for example, the learner keeps asking "but do we really need accountability?" in new forms).

**Pass:** within the next turn, the partner
1. states the core idea in one or two plain sentences,
2. researches the foundational question if one is at the center (with citations),
3. gives a provisional conclusion and names at most two genuinely open questions.

**Fail:** the partner asks another question without stating a conclusion, or gives a fifth example of the same unstated idea.

Origin: in the first trial the learner said the discussion was "going in circles" and asked for a concrete conclusion. That turn is the canonical failing case.
