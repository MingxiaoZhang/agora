# Diagrams (planned)

AI-generated visuals that support thinking, not replace it.

Candidates:
- **Argument map** of a unit: claims, assumptions, and evidence, drawn after the learner has stated their own view.
- **Position map**: the learner's positions across sources and how they connect, built from `learner/positions.yaml` and `ideas/`.
- **Changed-my-mind timeline** from position histories.

Rule: a diagram of the source's argument comes after the learner commits, never before. Showing the map first turns discovery into confirmation.
